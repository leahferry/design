About Me

Hi, I’m Leah Ferry, an interior designer passionate about creating immersive, experience-driven spaces that leave a lasting impact on the people who move through them. I’m especially interested in how interior design can shape emotion, interaction, and memory through atmosphere, movement, materiality, and sensory experience.

My approach to design is heavily influenced by spatial and architectural thinking, with a focus on creating environments that feel layered, engaging, and meaningful rather than simply functional. I’m drawn to spaces that encourage exploration, connection, and reflection through light, texture, form, and the way a space unfolds as someone moves through it. I believe the most impactful interiors are the ones that make people feel something.

Nature and the outdoors are a major source of inspiration in my work, influencing my use of organic forms, earthy textures, and biophilic elements to create spaces that feel calming, alive, and human-centered.

As a recent graduate of Kent State University with a Bachelor of Arts in Interior Design and a minor in Construction Management, I’m excited to continue exploring the intersection of immersive design, storytelling, and the built environment.
